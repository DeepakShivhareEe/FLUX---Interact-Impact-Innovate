### FLUX Ideathon Problem Statement Bank - Part 1 of 5
Problems PS-01 to PS-10 out of 50 curated statements. These problems are published for Ideathon teams, but they double as portfolio-ready project ideas across AI, IoT, healthcare, agriculture, education, safety, sustainability, and open innovation.

#### PS-01. Automated Scientific Literature Synthesis Engine
- **Domain:** AI for Research / NLP
- **Problem Statement:** Researchers face information overload with the exponential growth of academic papers, making it difficult to quickly synthesize state-of-the-art findings, compare methodologies, and identify research gaps manually.
- **Expected Solution:** Architect a sophisticated NLP-based Research Assistant capable of ingesting and parsing multiple academic papers to produce a structured comparative analysis. The system should automatically extract, correlate, and tabulate key methodologies, experimental results, and research gaps, effectively synthesizing a “State of the Art” summary that significantly reduces the time required for literature review.

#### PS-02. AI-Powered Indoor Fire Prediction Device
- **Domain:** Safety Engineering / IoT Systems
- **Problem Statement:** Traditional fire alarms only react after smoke or fire is present. There is a need for a predictive system that can detect pre-ignition conditions to prevent disasters before they start.
- **Expected Solution:** Architect a Multi-Modal Fire Prevention System fusing data from thermal, smoke, and gas sensors. The solution must utilize a predictive AI model to identify pre-ignition conditions or thermal runaway, dispatching instantaneous alerts to local fire departments and building occupants to prevent catastrophic incidents.

#### PS-03. AI-Driven Lost & Found Identification System
- **Domain:** Campus Automation / Vision AI
- **Problem Statement:** Lost items are often unclaimed in public places due to improper categorization and identification.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a smart kiosk where users upload images of lost items; AI vision models match appearance patterns with found items and notify users.

#### PS-04. Real-Time Personalized Analogy Generator
- **Domain:** EdTech + Generative AI
- **Problem Statement:** Complex academic concepts are often abstract and difficult to grasp, and educators lack tools to instantly generate personalized, relatable explanations that align with a student's specific interests.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a Context-Aware Generative AI Application where users input complex academic concepts, and the engine dynamically generates explanatory analogies mapped to their specific interests (e.g., sports, gaming). The output should be a text-based simplification tailored to the learner's cognitive preference, making abstract theories immediately relatable.

#### PS-05. AI-Powered Classroom Attention & Fatigue Analyzer
- **Domain:** Educational AI / Computer Vision
- **Problem Statement:** Educators lack real-time, objective data on student engagement and fatigue during lectures, making it difficult to adapt teaching strategies dynamically without using invasive surveillance methods.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop a Privacy-First Computer Vision System that estimates aggregate class engagement by analyzing student posture and movement patterns, strictly without using facial recognition. The system should render a real-time Engagement Heatmap, providing instructors with immediate feedback on lecture effectiveness and student fatigue levels.

#### PS-06. AI-Integrated Smart Farming Pest Detection Node
- **Domain:** AgriTech
- **Problem Statement:** Farmers face crop losses due to delayed identification of pests and plant diseases.
- **Expected Solution:** Design a portable IoT camera module that scans leaves, runs lightweight AI models on-device, and alerts farmers about detected pests or diseases through a mobile interface.

#### PS-07. System to Detect Early Signs of Nutrient Deficiencies
- **Domain:** Health AI
- **Problem Statement:** Early signs of nutritional deficiencies are often visible physically but overlooked due to a lack of accessible, non-invasive screening tools, leading to preventable long-term health issues.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a Computer Vision Diagnostic Tool capable of analyzing user-uploaded images for dermatological signs of deficiency, such as skin pallor or nail discoloration. The system should provide preliminary health insights and dietary recommendations, serving as an accessible first-step screening tool for nutritional health.

#### PS-08. AI for Hyper-Efficient Energy Management
- **Domain:** Energy AI / IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Institutional buildings suffer from massive energy wastage due to inefficient, manual control of lighting and heavy appliances in unoccupied or partially used zones.
- **Expected Solution:** Architect a Smart Energy Automation System that leverages occupancy detection algorithms to autonomously control lighting and appliances. The solution should visualize real-time consumption metrics and calculate potential cost savings via an analytical dashboard, helping institutions reduce their carbon footprint and electricity overheads.

#### PS-09. Smart Pet Health & Activity Tracker
- **Domain:** Animal Care / Wearable IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Pet owners often miss early signs of illness or behavioral changes due to lack of continuous monitoring.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a lightweight pet collar with sensors to track movement, heart rate, temperature, and sleep cycles. Use AI models to detect abnormal patterns and send alerts to owners via a mobile dashboard.

#### PS-10. Drone-Based Micro Delivery for Campus Logistics
- **Domain:** Drone Tech / Robotics
- **Problem Statement:** Intra-campus logistics for moving documents, lab samples, or small equipment are slow and labor-intensive, requiring a secure, autonomous solution for rapid point-to-point transport.
- **Expected Solution:** Simulate or prototype an Autonomous Aerial Delivery System designed for the secure, short-range transport of lightweight items within a geofenced campus environment. The system should feature live status telemetry and safety protocols to automate the movement of documents or samples between buildings.
