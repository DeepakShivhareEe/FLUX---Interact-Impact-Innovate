**URL Path:** /ideathon
**Source:** Frontend/src/projects.json

### FLUX Ideathon Problem Statement Bank - Part 5 of 5
Problems PS-41 to PS-50 out of 50 curated statements. These problems are published for Ideathon teams, but they double as portfolio-ready project ideas across AI, IoT, healthcare, agriculture, education, safety, sustainability, and open innovation.

#### PS-41. Mentor Connect: Smart Mentor–Student Matching & Progress Dashboard
- **Domain:** Career Development & Education Technology
- **Problem Statement:** Build a web platform that intelligently connects students with suitable mentors based on skills, goals, and learning style, while offering progress tracking, session management, and personalized guidance insights.
- **Expected Solution:** A system with mentor–student matching algorithms, user profiles, scheduling tools, goal-tracking dashboards, feedback analytics, and communication modules integrated into a clean, responsive web interface.

#### PS-42. AI-Driven Food Expiry & Nutrition Analyzer for Households
- **Domain:** Smart Kitchen / Consumer IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Families often waste food due to forgetting expiry dates and lack of nutritional awareness for daily meals.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a kitchen assistant using barcode scanning or image recognition to detect food items, predict freshness levels, track expiry, and suggest nutritional meal plans based on inventory.

#### PS-43. Predictive Women Safety Alert & Risk Detection System
- **Domain:** Public Safety / Mobile AI
- **Problem Statement:** Women’s safety is at risk due to delayed access to help and inability to detect high-risk situations early.
- **Expected Solution:** Build an AI-powered personal safety app using GPS, audio cues, motion sensors, and environmental data to predict unsafe zones or situations. The system triggers alerts, shares live location, and connects to emergency contacts automatically.

#### PS-44. Urban Heat Island Mapping Device + Cooling Strategy Planner
- **Domain:** Climate Tech / Smart City Hardware
- **Problem Statement:** Rapid urban heating goes undetected due to absence of localized temperature and humidity sensing networks.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a distributed sensor node (temperature, humidity, light reflectance, surface heat sensors) placed across an area. All nodes send real-time data to a web GIS dashboard that maps heat islands and recommends cooling strategies like shade nets, cool pavements, and green roofs.

#### PS-45. Smart Water Distribution Digital Twin + Sensor Network
- **Domain:** Smart Infrastructure Hardware
- **Problem Statement:** Water networks lack real-time visibility into pressure, leakage, and flow variations.
- **Expected Solution:** Deploy pressure, flow, and leak detection sensors across pipelines. Data is transmitted to a digital-twin web interface that simulates real-time flow, predicts leaks, and suggests optimized valve operations.

#### PS-46. Chemical Spill Detection & Toxicity Monitoring Kit
- **Domain:** Industrial Safety Hardware
- **Problem Statement:** Chemical spills require immediate detection and prediction, but current response is manual and slow.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a portable spill-detection device equipped with gas sensors, thermal sensors, and chemical reaction readings. The device sends data to a web application that predicts toxicity radius, vapor cloud formation, and safety perimeters.

#### PS-47. Smart Machine Health Monitoring Sensor Hub
- **Domain:** Manufacturing Hardware / Industrial IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Machines fail unexpectedly because vibration, load, and temperature data are not monitored continuously.
- **Expected Solution:** Install edge devices on machines that measure vibration, temperature, rpm, torque, and cycle count. Data syncs to a web portal that visualizes machine health, predicts failures, and benchmarks efficiency.

#### PS-48. Construction Safety Wearable + Site Monitoring Station
- **Domain:** Safety Hardware / Smart Wearables
- **Problem Statement:** Construction sites lack active safety monitoring, resulting in avoidable accidents.
- **Expected Solution:** Provide workers with a wearable safety device that tracks fall events, fatigue levels, gas exposure, and PPE compliance. A central site safety hub collects data and displays risk analytics on a web dashboard.

#### PS-49. Physical Stress & Material Failure Testing Device + Web Visualizer
- **Domain:** Digital Simulation / Material Testing Hardware
- **Problem Statement:** Students and designers cannot physically test material failure without expensive labs.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a small-scale tensile/bending testing device with sensors for load and deformation. A web portal visualizes stress-strain curves, failure modes, deformation animation, and performance metrics.

#### PS-50. Open Innovation Challenge
- **Domain:** Open Category (Hardware / Software / Hybrid)
- **Problem Statement:** Participants are invited to identify a significant real-world problem within their community, campus, or industry and engineer an innovative solution using any technological stack without domain constraints.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop a fully functional prototype that effectively addresses the identified problem. The solution should demonstrate practical feasibility, scalability, and measurable impact, utilizing any combination of AI, IoT, robotics, or software to deliver tangible value to the target user base.
