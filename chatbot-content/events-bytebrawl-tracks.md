### ByteBrawl Tracks & Problem Statement Details
The 2026 edition ran **25 verified problem statements across 5 India-first tracks**. Each team was allotted one problem statement for the final round.

#### Track: Satya & Pramaan Bharat (Truth & AI Verification)
Verifiable court judgment citations, reproducible AI test protocols, multi-lingual Indian LLM safety audits, and autonomous payment limit governors.

- **PS 1.1: Nyaya Pramaan — Check Quotes from Court Judgments** - built by *SqaudX*
- **PS 1.2: Bol-Kasauti — Test AI Safety in Indian Languages** - built by *Merged: Team Agastya + Byte Me*
- **PS 1.3: Parakh Patra — AI Test Results Others Can Repeat** - built by *PAIR PROGRAMMERS*
- **PS 1.4: Bhasha Kar — Compare AI Language Costs and Safety** - built by *ThinkAsCoder*
- **PS 1.5: Agent Lagam — Keep AI Payments Within Limits** - built by *CodeVortex*
