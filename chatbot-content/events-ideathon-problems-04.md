### FLUX Ideathon Problem Statement Bank - Part 4 of 5
Problems PS-31 to PS-40 out of 50 curated statements. These problems are published for Ideathon teams, but they double as portfolio-ready project ideas across AI, IoT, healthcare, agriculture, education, safety, sustainability, and open innovation.

#### PS-31. Gamified Eco-Behavior Monitoring in Schools
- **Domain:** Environmental Education
- **Problem Statement:** Students lack motivation to consistently follow eco-friendly practices.
- **Expected Solution:** Create IoT sensors tracking energy usage, water usage, and waste levels; convert these metrics into rewards/points on a gamified mobile app to drive behavior change.

#### PS-32. Smart Counterfeit Medicine Detector
- **Domain:** Healthcare / Quality Control
- **Problem Statement:** Fake or degraded medicines are hard to identify visually.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a handheld device using spectral sensors + AI models that identify anomalies in pill composition or packaging, and report quality scores on a connected app.

#### PS-33. AI-Based Unauthorized Construction Monitoring System
- **Domain:** Urban Governance / Smart City
- **Problem Statement:** Illegal constructions go unnoticed until they become major regulatory issues.
- **Expected Solution:** Use drone-captured or CCTV images and ML models to detect new structural changes; raise real-time alerts to city authorities.

#### PS-34. AI-Powered Indoor Air Quality & Health Safety Node
- **Domain:** Smart Home / Environmental Safety
- **Problem Statement:** Indoor pollution (CO₂, VOCs, humidity) is rising but often goes unnoticed.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a multi-sensor air monitoring device that uses AI to classify safe/unsafe conditions and control fans, purifiers, or send alerts.

#### PS-35. Intelligent Energy Theft Detection in Households
- **Domain:** Energy / Smart Grid
- **Problem Statement:** Electricity theft leads to massive revenue loss and unstable supply.
- **Expected Solution:** Build smart energy meters using IoT + ML anomaly detection to identify irregular consumption and report suspicious patterns to providers.

#### PS-36. Blockchain-Backed Seed Authenticity Ledger
- **Domain:** Blockchain + Agriculture
- **Problem Statement:** The agricultural supply chain is often compromised by counterfeit or low-quality seeds, causing significant financial losses to farmers who lack a verifiable method to confirm product origin and certification.
- **Expected Solution:** Implement a Supply Chain Verification System utilizing blockchain technology. The solution should allow farmers to scan QR codes on seed packets to retrieve an immutable history of origin, lab testing, and certification, ensuring they are purchasing authentic, high-quality seeds and protecting them from fraud.

#### PS-37. AI-Based Smart Parking Occupancy & Violation Detection
- **Domain:** Smart Mobility
- **Problem Statement:** Vehicles often park in no-parking zones or block entries due to lack of monitoring.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop an edge-based vision system that uses cameras + AI to detect empty parking slots, illegal parking, and send alerts to authorities or users in real-time.

#### PS-38. Smart Farming–Highway Synergy Network
- **Domain:** Logistics / AgriTech
- **Problem Statement:** Small-scale farmers struggle with high transport costs for produce, while highway logistics trucks often return empty after deliveries, creating a massive inefficiency in the supply chain.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a Logistics Aggregation Platform connecting farmers with highway transport operators. The marketplace should facilitate the booking of empty cargo space on trucks returning from deliveries, providing farmers with affordable transport for their produce while optimizing the utilization of logistics fleets.

#### PS-39. Personalized Career & Skill Growth Advisor
- **Domain:** EdTech / AI Guidance Systems
- **Problem Statement:** Students and young professionals struggle to identify skill gaps and choose suitable career paths based on industry demand.
- **Expected Solution:** Create an AI-driven platform that analyzes user skills, interests, performance data, and job market trends to generate personalized career paths, recommended courses, and skill development roadmaps.

#### PS-40. AI-Powered Clothing Damage Classifier & Repair Recommendation System
- **Domain:** RepairTech & Sustainable Fashion
- **Problem Statement:** Clothing items often get minor damages like small tears, fading, pilling, or loose stitches. Most users cannot identify the damage type or decide whether to repair, recycle, or replace the garment, leading to unnecessary textile waste.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a web-based platform where users upload images of damaged clothes. An AI model classifies the damage type and severity, suggests repair methods (DIY or professional), estimates repair cost, recommends nearby tailors, and gives fabric-specific preventive tips.
