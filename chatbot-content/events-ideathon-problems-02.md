### FLUX Ideathon Problem Statement Bank - Part 2 of 5
Problems PS-11 to PS-20 out of 50 curated statements. These problems are published for Ideathon teams, but they double as portfolio-ready project ideas across AI, IoT, healthcare, agriculture, education, safety, sustainability, and open innovation.

#### PS-11. AI Tool for Learning Endangered Micro-Dialects
- **Domain:** Linguistics + AI / Cultural Heritage
- **Problem Statement:** Indigenous languages and micro-dialects are disappearing rapidly, and current AI models require massive datasets that do not exist for these low-resource languages, making preservation difficult.
- **Expected Solution:** Architect a Few-Shot Learning NLP Model capable of training on extremely limited audio or text datasets. The system should generate educational narratives, vocabulary guides, and pronunciation aids to assist indigenous communities in preserving and revitalizing endangered dialects that lack large-scale digital resources.

#### PS-12. Smart Hospital Resource & Bed Availability Tracker
- **Domain:** HealthTech / Civic Tech
- **Problem Statement:** The lack of centralized, real-time visibility into hospital bed and critical resource availability across a city leads to critical delays in patient admission and emergency response.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop a Centralized Hospital Inventory Dashboard that allows medical staff to update bed occupancy and resource status in real-time. This provides patients and emergency responders with a transparent, live view of resource availability across multiple facilities, reducing critical delays in patient admission.

#### PS-13. AI-Driven Multi-Sensor Smart Cane for Visually Impaired
- **Domain:** Assistive Tech / Embedded Systems
- **Problem Statement:** Visually impaired individuals face significant safety risks from dynamic obstacles (vehicles) and overhead hazards that traditional canes cannot detect.
- **Expected Solution:** Design an Intelligent Assistive Mobility Device integrating LiDAR and ultrasonic sensor arrays. The system must process environmental data via an embedded AI model to detect dynamic obstacles and overhead hazards, providing real-time haptic and auditory guidance to the user while syncing telemetry with a companion mobile application.

#### PS-14. Intelligent AI-Based Waste Sorting Bin
- **Domain:** Smart Cities / Environmental Engineering
- **Problem Statement:** Manual waste segregation is inefficient and error-prone, leading to lower recycling rates and overflowing public bins due to a lack of real-time monitoring.
- **Expected Solution:** Engineer an Automated Waste Segregation Unit utilizing computer vision and load cells to classify refuse into recyclable categories. The system should feature a cloud-connected dashboard for real-time fill-level monitoring and route optimization for municipal collection services, promoting efficient urban sanitation.

#### PS-15. AI-Enabled Smart Helmet for Accident Prevention
- **Domain:** Transportation Safety / IoT Hardware
- **Problem Statement:** Two-wheeler accidents have high fatality rates due to delayed emergency response and a lack of mechanisms to detect dangerous driving behaviors or falls in real-time.
- **Expected Solution:** Prototype a Smart Safety Helmet embedded with IMU, alcohol sensors, and GPS modules. The device must utilize an edge-AI algorithm to detect erratic driving patterns or high-impact collisions, automatically triggering a distress signal with precise geolocation to emergency responders and designated contacts.

#### PS-16. IoT + AI Based Smart Water Quality Guardian
- **Domain:** Rural Development / Environmental IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Rural communities lack access to affordable, real-time water quality testing, leading to the consumption of unsafe water and the spread of waterborne diseases.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop a Portable IoT Hydro-Analytics Node capable of measuring critical parameters like turbidity and pH. The system should employ a machine learning classifier to determine water potability in real-time, transmitting contamination trends to a centralized community health dashboard for proactive intervention.

#### PS-17. Reality Drift Detector – Spot Manipulated Real-World Scenes
- **Domain:** AI Security / Deepfake Detection
- **Problem Statement:** The proliferation of hyper-realistic AI-generated media poses a severe threat to information integrity, creating an urgent need for accessible forensic tools to verify the authenticity of digital visual content.
- **Expected Solution:** Engineer a Deepfake Detection Framework capable of analyzing visual media for minute forensic anomalies, such as lighting inconsistencies, unnatural distortions, or pixel artifacts. The interface should highlight potentially manipulated regions and provide a confidence score regarding the media's authenticity, helping users distinguish between genuine and synthetic content.

#### PS-18. Smart AI-Driven Traffic Light System
- **Domain:** Smart Transportation / Embedded AI
- **Problem Statement:** Static traffic timers contribute to congestion and often delay emergency vehicles. A dynamic system is needed to adjust signal timing based on real-time traffic density.
- **Expected Solution:** Construct an Adaptive Traffic Control System leveraging computer vision and infrared sensors to analyze vehicular density. The AI engine should dynamically modulate signal timing cycles to minimize congestion latency and prioritize the transit of emergency vehicles through intersections.

#### PS-19. AI-Powered Early Detection of Structural Cracks
- **Domain:** Civil Engineering / IoT Sensors
- **Problem Statement:** Infrastructure failures often occur without warning because structural fatigue and micro-cracks are difficult to detect during manual visual inspections.
- **Expected Solution:** Design a Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) Node that analyzes vibration frequencies and acoustic resonance patterns. The system should deploy an anomaly detection algorithm to identify early signs of material fatigue or micro-cracks, generating predictive maintenance reports for civil engineering authorities.

#### PS-20. Smart Agriculture Pod with Automated Spray Control
- **Domain:** Agriculture / Robotics
- **Problem Statement:** Broad-spectrum pesticide spraying wastes chemicals and harms the environment. Farmers need a precision tool to detect and treat only the specific plants affected by pests.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a Precision Agriculture Robotic Unit equipped with multispectral cameras and soil sensors. The device must utilize computer vision to identify specific pest infestations and execute targeted micro-spraying operations, thereby reducing chemical usage and optimizing crop health through localized treatment.
