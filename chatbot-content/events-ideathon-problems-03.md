### FLUX Ideathon Problem Statement Bank - Part 3 of 5
Problems PS-21 to PS-30 out of 50 curated statements. These problems are published for Ideathon teams, but they double as portfolio-ready project ideas across AI, IoT, healthcare, agriculture, education, safety, sustainability, and open innovation.

#### PS-21. AI-Assisted Real-Time Sign Language to Speech Converter
- **Domain:** Assistive Hardware / Wearable Tech
- **Problem Statement:** People with speech impairments often face communication barriers. There is a need for a wearable device that translates sign language gestures into audible speech instantly.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a Wearable Sensory Glove integrated with flex sensors and accelerometers to capture hand kinematics. The system should process these gesture data streams through a classification model to convert Sign Language into audible speech in real-time, bridging communication gaps for the speech-impaired.

#### PS-22. Smart Campus Environmental Quality Monitoring Station
- **Domain:** Environmental Hardware / Smart Campus Tech
- **Problem Statement:** Campuses cannot continuously track pollution, wastewater quality, and noise levels.
- **Expected Solution:** Deploy environmental sensing stations across the campus that measure air quality, water pH, noise, and waste levels. Data is streamed to a web dashboard for compliance alerts and long-term environmental trend analysis.

#### PS-23. AI-Based Smart Drowsiness Detector for Workers
- **Domain:** Industrial Safety / Biomedical AI
- **Problem Statement:** Industrial accidents are frequently caused by worker fatigue or microsleeps, which are difficult to supervise manually in high-risk environments.
- **Expected Solution:** Engineer a Wearable Industrial Safety Device capable of monitoring physiological markers such as pulse rate and blink frequency. The system must employ a fatigue detection algorithm to identify microsleeps or exhaustion, triggering haptic alerts to the worker and safety logs to supervisors.

#### PS-24. Flood Prediction & Automated Village Siren System
- **Domain:** Disaster Management / IoT
- **Problem Statement:** Vulnerable riverbank communities often receive flood warnings too late. A localized, automated early warning system is needed to trigger alerts based on real-time water levels.
- **Expected Solution:** Construct a Solar-Powered Hydrological Station that tracks water levels and flow velocity. The system should leverage a predictive model to forecast flood risks and automatically activate local siren networks while broadcasting SMS evacuation alerts to vulnerable communities.

#### PS-25. AI-Powered Smart Cooking Gas Leak Detector
- **Domain:** Household Safety / Embedded Systems
- **Problem Statement:** Domestic gas leaks are a major fire hazard, and standard alarms often trigger false positives or lack the ability to actively shut off the gas supply.
- **Expected Solution:** Design a Smart Kitchen Safety Node integrating gas concentration and thermal sensors. The AI logic must distinguish between minor leaks, cooking fumes, and critical hazards, automatically actuating a solenoid valve to cut off the gas supply and notifying users via a mobile interface.

#### PS-26. Web-Based Intelligent Clothing Lifespan & Repair Recommendation System
- **Domain:** Sustainable Fashion / Consumer Web Technology
- **Problem Statement:** Fast fashion leads to premature disposal of garments due to lack of awareness about repairability or actual wear condition. Users often discard clothes that could be repaired or upcycled, contributing to textile waste.
- **Expected Solution:** Build a web platform where users upload clothing images or enter garment details. The system should assess visible wear patterns, estimate remaining lifespan, and recommend repair, maintenance, or upcycling options. It should also help users track wardrobe health over time and connect them with local repair or tailoring services

#### PS-27. AI-Based Smart Kirana Store Shelf Manager
- **Domain:** Retail Automation
- **Problem Statement:** Small shops struggle with inventory visibility, expired stock, and restocking delays.
- **Expected Solution:** Develop an IoT camera + AI module that detects low stock, tracks expiration, and sends auto-restock alerts to shopkeepers or suppliers through a mobile app.

#### PS-28. Intelligent Asset Tracking & Anti-Theft Node
- **Domain:** Logistics / IoT Security
- **Problem Statement:** Valuable items in campuses or offices frequently go missing due to lack of real-time tracking.
- **Expected Solution:** Create a compact IoT tag with BLE + GPS + motion sensing; use AI anomaly detection to identify unusual movement patterns and trigger alerts.

#### PS-29. MicroLoan Risk Predictor for Small Vendors
- **Domain:** FinTech / Social Impact
- **Problem Statement:** Unbanked street vendors and small businesses lack formal credit histories/collateral, excluding them from financial systems despite having consistent digital transaction footprints.
- **Expected Solution:** Architect an Alternative Credit Scoring Algorithm that evaluates digital transaction history and operational consistency rather than traditional collateral. The model should generate a "Trust Score" to assist financial institutions in assessing micro-loan eligibility for unbanked street vendors, fostering financial inclusion.

#### PS-30. Smart Attendance & Face Analytics System for Rural Schools
- **Domain:** EdTech / Computer Vision
- **Problem Statement:** Manual attendance is time-consuming and prone to manipulation in low-resource schools.
- **Expected Solution:** Design a low-cost edge device with a camera for automated presence detection, face recognition, and real-time attendance updates to teachers’ dashboards.
